## Notable Satellite Projects (Recent)

- **NASA Earth Observing System (EOS) Senior Project Scientist**
- **MODIS Atmosphere Team Lead**
- **MODIS Cloud Optical Products Lead** — The GSFC Cloud Retrieval Group enhances, maintains, and evaluates existing MODIS Terra and Aqua Atmosphere Team algorithms currently being produced by the GSFC MODIS Adaptive Processing System (MODAPS). These global products (cloud thermodynamic phase, optical thickness, effective particle radius, water path, retrieval uncertainties) include pixel level and gridded (spatial/temporal) products, including specialized gridded datasets for model evaluation (IPCC CFMIP COSP datasets and satellite simulator).
- **Suomi NPP/VIIRS Atmosphere Team, Cloud Product Algorithm Development and Refinement** — While critical for climate studies, multidecadal continuity of cloud data records between MODIS and VIIRS is problematic because of the absence of key spectral channels on VIIRS and a significant change in the spectral location for a key shortwave infrared band used for cloud microphysical retrievals. This project developed, and continues to maintain, a suite of algorithms that can be applied to the spectral channels common to the two imagers.
